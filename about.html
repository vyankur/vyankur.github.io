About Me

Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Professional

Hello, I'm Ankur, and I have over 11 years of experience in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, specializing in Tableau, SQL, data visualization, and enterprise reporting.

Throughout my career, I've worked across insurance, banking, and enterprise analytics, partnering with business stakeholders to transform complex data into actionable insights.

My expertise spans the complete analytics lifecycle—from understanding business requirements and designing data models to developing dashboards, optimizing SQL, and delivering executive reports that support strategic decision-making.

In my current role at Sun Life, I develop enterprise BI solutions using Tableau, SQL, AWS Athena, and Redshift. I work closely with cross-functional teams to define KPIs, improve reporting efficiency, ensure data quality, and deliver scalable analytics solutions. Specifically, my key accomplishments include:

  • Platform Architecture: Built an enterprise-wide insurance analytics platform spanning HNW, attrition, adoption, and product performance use cases. Designed a custom adoption dashboard suite used by VPs, Product Owners, Change Managers, and BU Heads, driving a 30% increase in platform adoption across business units.
  • Customer Experience: Developed executive dashboards integrating client feedback from Qualtrics, Salesforce, and social media channels across customer journeys, achieving a 20% increase in customer satisfaction index.
  • Performance Optimization: Optimized dashboard performance using Tableau Performance Recording, query tuning, and a balanced live/extract data strategy.
  • Governance & Security: Delivered advanced financial reporting with entitlement-based row-level security (RLS), complex LOD calculations, and thorough null handling for executive reporting accuracy.
  • Administration: Managed Tableau Server and Cloud administration, permissions, scheduling, deployment automation, and release processes across Dev, UAT, and Production.
  • AI/ML POC: Led a proof-of-concept using the BMAD Framework to orchestrate AI agents for automated dashboard requirement gathering and spec generation, streamlining the analytics intake process.
  • Compliance Validation POC: Designed an AI-powered Proof of Compliance Validation system using Amazon SageMaker, Bedrock, and Claude Vision to automate assessment of regulatory evidence across structured (Excel) and unstructured (PDFs, scanned documents, images) data. Built an end-to-end pipeline with S3 ingestion, document chunking, semantic retrieval via embeddings, and multimodal foundation models to classify submissions into Compliant, Partially Compliant, and Non-Compliant categories with explainable results, significantly reducing manual document review effort.

Previously, at HCL, I worked on BI projects where I gained strong experience in stakeholder management, report automation, and delivering solutions in Agile environments:

  • Financial Planning: Led a team of 2 developers to build a 5-year financial planning and budgeting dashboard suite for Deutsche Bank, covering inter- and intra-department spend analysis across programs, delivered exclusively for senior leadership and CXO-level decision-makers.
  • ESG Compliance: Built compliance dashboards to track sustainability metrics, governance scores, and disclosure requirements across the bank's investment portfolio.
  • Data Integration & Security: Connected Tableau live to SAP HANA for high-performance query execution, used Databricks/PySpark for ETL, and applied row-level security (RLS) to restrict data access by department and user roles.

I have also contributed to HR Analytics and business reporting projects, building dashboards that enabled leaders to monitor workforce and operational metrics through interactive visualizations.

One of my key strengths is my ability to bridge the gap between business and technology. I enjoy collaborating with stakeholders, understanding their challenges, and translating them into practical, data-driven solutions that create measurable business value.

I'm excited about this opportunity because it aligns perfectly with my experience in Business Intelligence, analytics delivery, stakeholder engagement, and continuous learning. I look forward to contributing my technical expertise and business-focused approach to help the organization make better data-driven decisions.
